# immune.brand.profanity

## What it is for

Brand-safe assistants whose replies must never swear.

## What it deliberately leaves alone

The same words in titles, place names and historical names, quotes and exact repetitions the user asked for, and explanations of what a word means or where it comes from. The word list finds candidates and Jev confirms each one, so innocent uses pass while the list stays short.

## References

Brand-safety guidelines for customer-facing assistants commonly forbid profanity.
