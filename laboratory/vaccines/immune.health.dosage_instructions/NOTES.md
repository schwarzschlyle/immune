# immune.health.dosage_instructions

## What it is for

Assistants that sell or talk about medicines and supplements without being a clinical service, such as retail, travel and HR assistants. Telling a person how much to take is clinical advice.

## What it deliberately leaves alone

Product, stock, price and storage information; what a leaflet says without turning it into an instruction; the strength a doctor already prescribed; and pointing people to a pharmacist or doctor. It is not meant for clinical services, where dosing is the job: leave it off there.

## References

Retail and general-purpose assistants are not licensed to give individual dosing advice; pharmacy regulators expect it from a pharmacist or prescriber.
