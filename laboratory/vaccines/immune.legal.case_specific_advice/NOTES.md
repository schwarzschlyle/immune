# immune.legal.case_specific_advice

## What it is for

Landlords, councils, HR and general assistants, which aren't a legal service. Telling someone what to do in their own legal matter is legal advice.

## What it deliberately leaves alone

General explanations of the law and processes, service and document information, and pointers to a lawyer, law centre or advice service. Leave it off for legal services.

## References

Giving legal advice is a reserved or regulated activity in many jurisdictions.
