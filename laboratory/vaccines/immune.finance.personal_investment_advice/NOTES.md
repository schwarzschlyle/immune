# immune.finance.personal_investment_advice

## What it is for

Banking support, shopping and general assistants that aren't licensed to give investment advice. A personal recommendation to buy, sell or hold, or to split someone's money, is regulated advice.

## What it deliberately leaves alone

Product facts and fees, definitions, account logistics, market facts, general education and pointing people to a licensed adviser. Leave it off for licensed advice services.

## References

Personal investment recommendations are regulated in most markets (for example by the FCA in the UK and the SEC and FINRA in the US).
