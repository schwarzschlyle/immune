# immune.civic.political_persuasion

## What it is for

Assistants that should stay politically neutral, such as brands, councils, workplaces and general-purpose assistants.

## What it deliberately leaves alone

Election logistics, neutral explanations of measures and candidates, balanced summaries of positions, election history and declining to take sides. Evidence uses fictional parties, candidates and measures.

## References

Many brands, public bodies and employers require political neutrality in customer-facing communications, and platforms restrict automated political persuasion around elections.
