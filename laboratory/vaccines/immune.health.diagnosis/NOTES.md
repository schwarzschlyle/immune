# immune.health.diagnosis

## What it is for

Assistants that hear about symptoms but are not a clinical service: pharmacy shops, HR and general assistants. Telling a person what condition they have is a diagnosis.

## What it deliberately leaves alone

General health information that isn't about the user, service and booking information, and urging people to see a doctor or call emergency services (urgent safety advice is always allowed). Leave it off for clinical triage services.

## References

Diagnosis is regulated clinical activity in most jurisdictions; general assistants should direct people to a clinician.
